# PlayStation Portable

> **Documentation only.** This directory holds notes, folder layouts and
> checklists. It contains no ROMs, disc images, BIOS dumps or encryption keys,
> it links to none, and it explains where to obtain none. Every entry below
> assumes you are supplying a dump you made yourself from media you own, which
> is the only use this guide supports.


- **Emulator:** PPSSPP Gold (paid)
- **Games go in:** `SD_TEMPLATE/ROMs/psp/`
- **BIOS:** None. PPSSPP requires no BIOS dump.

## Notes

PPSSPP Gold is the paid build — the same emulator, and buying it is how the
developers get paid for something this good.

Internal resolution 3x-4x. PSP emulation is essentially solved on this
hardware.

Two things beyond the checklist: **multiplayer works** for many titles, ad-hoc
or online, and community patches exist for 60 fps and second-analogue camera
control in some games (Liberty City Stories was reported). Those install as
cheats, so they are easy to apply and to undo.

**Do not rely on save states.**

Birth by Sleep FINAL MIX is the Japanese release; an English or Spanish version
means a fan translation over your own dump.
